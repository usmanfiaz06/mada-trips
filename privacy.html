Who we are

The Mada Trips app and madatrips.sa are run by Mada Travel and Tourism (“Mada”, “we”), a licensed travel agency in Riyadh, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. We are the controller of the personal data described here under the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and its Implementing Regulations.

  • Authorities, when Saudi law requires it.

Data stored outside Saudi Arabia

Today our main database is hosted in South Korea and some providers above are in other countries. Transfers outside the Kingdom are made under the PDPL’s rules on cross-border transfers: only what each provider needs, under contracts that require protection at least equal to Saudi law, and recorded in our transfer assessment. Passport numbers are encrypted before they are stored, with keys we hold. We plan to move the database to a Saudi region as one becomes available to us. Your rights

Under the PDPL you can:

  • Know what we hold and why (this page).
  • Get a copy: in the app, Profile › Privacy › Download everything. We email a link within 24 hours.
  • Correct your details: in the app, or ask us.
  • Delete your account: in the app, Profile › Privacy › Delete my account. See Deleting your account.
  • Withdraw consent for analytics and marketing at any time in Profile › Privacy, and for contacts or notifications in your phone’s settings.
  • Complain to the Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA) if you think we have not handled your data properly. We would like the chance to put it right first: privacy@madatrips.sa.

We answer requests within 30 days, usually much sooner.

How we protect it

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passport and ID numbers are encrypted in our database (AES-256-GCM) and shown in full only when needed. Only Mada staff working on your trips can see your bookings, and every access to documents and payments is recorded. Your sign-in is stored in your phone’s secure keychain, and you can lock the Wallet and the app with Face ID or a fingerprint.

If a breach puts your data at risk, we will tell you and SDAIA as the law requires.

Children

You must be 18 or older to have a Mada Trips account. Parents and guardians can add children to their household so we can book for them; the account holder is responsible for having the right to share their details.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the date at the top, and for important changes we tell you in the app before they take effect.
