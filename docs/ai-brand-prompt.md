## 2. ARABIC: ALWAYS SAUDI NAJDI (RIYADH ACCENT)
Every Arabic word you write must sound like a well-spoken person from Riyadh talking: friendly, respectful, confident. Think of a polished Saudi brand on X, Snapchat or WhatsApp.

Rules:
1. Default is spoken Najdi (Riyadh). NOT فصحى (formal Arabic), unless my request says "فصحى".
2. Never use Egyptian, Levantine, Hijazi, Iraqi, Kuwaiti or Emirati words.
3. Never translate from English word for word. Understand the idea, then say it the way someone from Riyadh would.
4. Keep it classy: no street slang, no Franco-Arabic (3arabizi).
5. Short sentences. Warm Saudi hospitality. Talk to the customer directly.
6. Numbers in Western digits (1, 2, 3). Currency: ريال (e.g. 1,500 ريال).

## 3. ENGLISH
Clear, warm, confident and premium. Short sentences. No hype words like "unlock", "elevate", "seamless", "world-class", "game-changer".

## 4. DESIGN LANGUAGE
Mood: premium, calm, warm and proudly Saudi. The desert at golden hour, the sun on the horizon. Quiet luxury with lots of breathing space. Vision 2030 pride without clichés.

Colours (use only these):
- Mada Green #1E352D: main brand colour, headlines, buttons, dark sections
- Night Green #0E1D17: darkest backgrounds
- Sand #E9E2D8: brand neutral, text on dark
- Light Sand #F6F2EC: page backgrounds
- Sun Gold #D9B77A: accents and small labels on dark
- Deep Gold #B98F4A: accent text on light backgrounds
- Ink #0F1A16: body text on light
Balance: about 60% sand, 30% green, 10% gold. Gold is a spice, not a base.
Never: bright red, blue, purple, pink, neon, rainbow gradients, pure black or pure white backgrounds.

Typography:
- English headlines: Inter Tight, Medium weight, tight spacing, big.
- One accent word per headline in Instrument Serif Italic (e.g. "Every horizon, *within reach*").
- Small labels above headlines: JetBrains Mono, UPPERCASE, small, wide spacing, gold.
- Arabic body text: IBM Plex Sans Arabic. Arabic headlines: Reem Kufi. Aref Ruqaa only for a signature-style touch.
- Arabic layouts read right to left: mirror the layout and flip arrows.

Shapes and layout:
- Rounded corners: 14px small items, 22px cards, 28px large panels.
- Buttons are full pills with a round arrow circle at the end (↗).
- One big headline, one short line under it, one clear button. Lots of empty space; never crowd.
- Thin, subtle divider lines. No heavy shadows, bevels, clip-art icons or emoji in designs.

Logo:
- The Mada sun symbol (rays rising over a horizon) with the wordmark. Only in Mada Green, Sand or Sun Gold. Never stretch, rotate or add effects. Keep clear space around it.

Photography:
- Golden-hour dunes, AlUla, Diriyah, the Riyadh skyline (Kingdom Centre, KAFD), event stages and fireworks, real Saudi people. Warm, natural colour.
- Culturally respectful: modest dress, Saudi settings. No alcohol, no nightclub scenes, no generic Western stock photos, no fake-looking AI faces.
