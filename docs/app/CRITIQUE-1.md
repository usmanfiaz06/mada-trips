### 1b. What the best do (patterns that transfer to a mobile booking + concierge app)

- **Flighty — "boringly obvious", airport-board language, 15 stages of a travel day.** Founder Ryan Jones: the aim is for the app to feel "almost boringly obvious", Live Activities "recall airport signage conventions"; the app "transitions through 15 distinct stages of a travel day" (weather at ~10 days, check-in codes at 24 h) and often beats the airline's own push. https://flighty.app/press , https://thesweetsetup.com/flightys-beautiful-design-fast-push-notifications-make-it-one-of-the-best-flight-tracking-apps-on-the-app-store/ , https://blakecrosley.com/guides/design/flighty . Transfer: the Today card must change state by time-to-departure, and the lock screen is the primary surface on travel day, not the app.
- **Google Flights — date grid and price graph.** Shows "the prices of all combinations of dates, three days either side", colour-coded, and a 60-day low-price graph; shifts the question from "what does this date cost" to "which dates are cheap". https://www.australianfrequentflyer.com.au/google-flights-guide . Its price guarantee pilot refunds the difference automatically if the fare drops ≥$5 before departure. https://thepointsguy.com/news/google-flights-price-guarantee . Transfer: for Eid/summer families with flexible days, a 7-day price strip beats "This weekend" chips.
- **Hopper — Price Freeze at the decision point.** A $1–40 deposit holds a fare 3–7 days, deposit credited if you book; "if the flight price you froze decreases, you will pay that lower price". https://help.hopper.com/en_us/price-freeze-for-flights-how-to-redeem-your-price-freeze-ry2fKOtv , https://thriftytraveler.com/hopper-launches-price-freeze-feature/ . The cautionary half: FTC order on pre-ticked fees and unreachable "VIP Support" https://natlawreview.com/article/travel-app-pay-35m-settle-ftc-allegations-it-charged-fees-without-consent . Transfer: a "hold this for the family meeting tonight" option (free, with the desk) is the honest version.
- **Airbnb — cancellation clarity as a conversion lever, trust before pay.** Hosts moving from Strict to Firm saw bookings rise ~9% in Airbnb's pilot https://news.airbnb.com/firm-cancellation-policy ; Airbnb agents "acknowledged that people struggle to understand the refund rules" when 14-day and 48-hour windows were combined https://abc7news.com/post/airbnb-refund-policy-called-unfair/5151985/ . Transfer: one plain sentence with a date ("Free until 3 Mar, then SAR 400") on every price, and on the trip afterwards.
- **Booking.com — the "Free cancellation" badge and family filters as trust signals; Genius as a visible tier.** https://apps.apple.com/app/booking-com-hotels-travel/id367003839 , https://travelnest.com/news-and-insights/boost-visibility-and-increase-bookings-with-booking-com-genius-program . Transfer: a badge on the card, not a sentence in the details.
- **Nusuk — the permit is the gating artefact.** Permits are mandatory, one per person per Ramadan, tied to a visa for visitors, with time slots and gate allocation https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/ask-us/how-to-get-an-umrah-permit-via-the-nusuk-app-1.500029500 . Transfer: an Umrah flow has to put the permit slot *first* and build hotel/train around it.
- **Apple HIG onboarding:** three screens at most, let people explore before an account, progressive disclosure https://skills.cat/skills/raintree-technology/apple-hig-skills/hig-patterns .
- **Baymard (flights, accommodations):** 240+ flight-booking guidelines, 87 desktop / 61 mobile checkout guidelines; the 2026 accommodation benchmark covers 8 mobile apps https://baymard.com/research/flight-booking-and-airlines , https://baymard.com/research-articles/travel-accommodations-ux-benchmark-2026 . One academic replication: "price invisibility of travel items was the issue that participants struggled with the most" https://theseus.fi/handle/10024/167904 .
- **Wroblewski on mobile forms:** "radio buttons, checkboxes, select menus and lists tend to work much better than open text fields"; keep dependent inputs structured https://static.lukew.com/MIX10_WebForms_Lukew_03152010.pdf , https://smashingmagazine.com/author/luke-wroblewski . One idea per page https://design.alberta.ca/patterns/simple-form .
- **Norman's gulfs:** execution = "the gap between what you want to do and what the system allows"; evaluation = a spinner that gives no sense of progress https://www.educative.io/answers/gulf-of-execution-and-gulf-of-evaluation . Mada's docs already cite these; the question in Part 2 is whether the live app honours them.
- **NN/g (via RESEARCH.md §3):** "less chat, more answer"; Amadeus 2026 test: for fixed tasks like rebooking users preferred screens to chat.

## Part 2 — Twelve scenarios on staging (notes as they were walked)

Shots: `scratchpad/critique/shots/`. "CM" = clueless moment (the person does not know what happened, what to do next, or what it will cost).

### Scenario 1 — First open, no account, just curious (`s1-*.png`)
Steps: open / → Welcome ("We'll take it from here. Say where. We book it and stay with you until you're home." Start · Track a flight · Got an invite code?) → Track a flight → guest Today → each tab → Start → Sign in (Google · email · phone).
- Good: guest mode exists and is honest ("No account needed"); the sign-in screen states "A real person checks every booking"; no pop-ups; three screens or fewer to a working app (HIG).
- CM-1.1 **A curious person cannot see a price or a hotel without an account.** Guest Ask shows chips ("Flights to Istanbul", "A hotel in Istanbul", "Umrah in Ramadan"); tapping any opens the sign-in sheet. Booking.com, Almosafer, Skyscanner all let you search and see prices anonymously; Mada asks for trust before giving anything. `s1-04-ask-guest.png`.
- CM-1.2 **Guest Trips is a decorative departures board** ("Your name's not on the board yet") with AlUla/London/Cairo flaps that do nothing when tapped; a first-timer reads it as content and gets nothing. `s1-05-trips-guest.png`.
- CM-1.3 **Guest Circles is an empty "Discover" with "On this week in " (city blank) and three empty headings.** The feed has no city, no tips, and no prompt to sign in. `s1-06-circles-guest.png`.
- CM-1.4 **Deep links to /plan/alula2, /hotel/<code> and /support render only "Home"** for a guest (blank page with a Home link; the support screen fires a 401). A shared plan link from a friend is the most likely first contact with Mada, and it is blank. `s1-08-plan-guest.png`, `s1-09-support-guest.png`.
- Nothing on the welcome or sign-in screen says what Mada costs, who Mada is (licence, IATA), or shows one real trip. The best competitor pattern here is Airbnb's "browse first, commit later" and Flighty's "the app is useful before you pay".
- Verdict: 4/10 for the curious visitor. Fix: let guests search flights and stays and see prices (sign-in at "slide to book"), fill the guest Discover with a real city, render plan/hotel/support links for guests, and show one line of credentials on Welcome ("Licensed Saudi agency · IATA · a named agent on every booking").

### Scenario 2 — A mother booking Eid in Istanbul for 2 adults + 3 children aged 2, 6, 13 (`s2*-*.png`)
Typed exactly: "Istanbul for Eid, 2 adults and 3 kids aged 2, 6 and 13".
- Good: "Eid" resolved to Eid al-Fitr 9–15 Mar 2027 (correct Umm al-Qura); a date chip "Eid al-Fitr · Mar 2027" and "School break" exist in the calendar; three options with a one-line reason each; the entry-rule check caught the 150-day Türkiye rule with a precise date; "Babies on a lap: under 2 on the day you fly back, one per adult" is well written.
- CM-2.1 **The whole family vanished.** The result header reads "1 adult · Economy", "holding seats for 1", price SAR 2,160. Nothing says "we couldn't read 2 adults and 3 kids". A mother reads SAR 2,160 as the family price. The parser (`packages/shared/src/schemas/booking-logic.ts:130-153 parseWho`) only recognises household members by name, "kids/children" when the household has kids, "baby/twins", and "just me"; counts like "2 adults and 3 kids" are dropped silently. `s2a-0.png`.
- CM-2.2 **Correcting it in the Ask bar fails with a hotel error.** Typing "we are 2 adults and 3 children, aged 2, 6 and 13" into "Tell Mada what matters: morning, direct" returns **"Mada didn't catch what to change. Try a place, a view, a pool, breakfast or a price."** — on a flight search. `s2f-1.png`.
- CM-2.3 **"Add someone" cannot add a child.** The sheet asks only "Given names · Surname · Family/Friend/Helper/Colleague · Add them". No date of birth, no "child/infant". Age comes only from a scanned passport (`dateOfBirth` is set by the passport flow, `people.ts:51`), so three children added by name are priced as adults until each passport is scanned, and the 2-year-old cannot be a lap infant via this path at all (the lap counter lives in a different sheet, Edit search). `s2b-1.png`, `s2d-1-s*.png`.
- CM-2.4 **The three "Best/Cheapest/Earliest" options ignore the children.** "Earliest" is a 02:10 departure with "A full first day, after a short night" — for a 2-year-old. No option mentions seats together, bassinet or child meals; the "Best for you" reason is "Direct. Lands before check-in." The ranking copy admits a family mode exists ("family: 2 children under 12", `booking.ts:229`) but it never fired because the family was never parsed.
- CM-2.5 **"Renew it first" turns into "Mada is on it" and nothing else.** A request is created (`/requests` POST) but the screen does not say what will happen, when, what it costs, or whether the flights are held meanwhile; the primary button still reads "Sort out Noura's passport first". The mother is stuck on this screen with no next step. `s2e-1.png`.
- CM-2.6 **"Add a room near Galata Tower and airport pickup? 6 nights · SAR 3,674 · Add"** — no hotel name, no room type, no "2 rooms / connecting", no per-night price, no cancellation rule; tapping Add turns it to "Added" and the total is not shown anywhere. For a family of five this is the single most expensive decision on the screen and it is a blind one. `s2i-1.png`.
- CM-2.7 "Tap again for bags and change rules" on the Best card did nothing on the second tap in the headless run (`s2g-2.png`); unverified whether a sheet opened under the hood, but the hint should be a visible "Rules" link, not a hidden double-tap.
- Competitor patterns to borrow: Google Flights' explicit traveller stepper (adults / children with ages / infants on lap vs seat) shown *before* results; Almosafer's and Booking's "family rooms" and "children's ages" prompts; Flighty's calm "we're missing X" line. Airline family-seating promises (DOT dashboard) show why "seats together" must be a visible line on the family card.
- Verdict: 3/10. The most common Saudi booking (a family at Eid) is the one the app parses worst, and it fails silently.

### Scenario 3 — A solo man, Umrah in Ramadan, has a Nusuk permit (`s3*-*.png`)
Typed: "Umrah in Ramadan, just me, I have a Nusuk permit".
- CM-3.1 **The first thing Mada says is "Every traveller needs their own Nusuk permit. We'll remind you to get them once dates are set."** He just said he has one. Then: "Anything we should arrange for each person? Wheelchair to the gate · Wheelchair to the seat · Diabetic meal · Low-salt meal · Soft food · Seat near the toilet · Travelling with oxygen · Nothing needed". A healthy man is greeted with a geriatric checklist as question one. `s3a-0.png`.
- CM-3.2 **No dates asked.** "Ramadan" was not turned into a date range (Ramadan 1448 ≈ 8 Feb–8 Mar 2027) and no "which nights" question appears; the flow goes Nothing needed → "Where to stay in Makkah? Steps from the Haram / Good value, short ride" → "Anything else we should know?" → Send to Mada. The permit date, which is the thing everything else must be built around (see Part 1c), is never requested. The last-ten-nights price cliff is not mentioned.
- CM-3.3 Nothing about Madinah, the Haramain train, or ihram/miqat; "Steps from the Haram" is the right instinct but it is a chip, not a promise ("within 300 m, we name the hotel before you pay"), which is the #1 Umrah complaint in Part 1a.
- Verdict: 4/10. Nusuk's pattern (permit slot first) and the Umrah package pricing already in the desk (`DESK_PRICES.umrah*`) should be surfaced as a dated, per-night quote before "Send".

### Scenario 6 — Landed, needs a car now, phone at 8% (`s6a-0.png`)
Typed: "I just landed at Istanbul airport, I need a car right now to Taksim".
- CM-6.1 The reply is **"From when? Tomorrow · This weekend · Next week · For my trip dates".** There is no "Now". "Right now" and "just landed" are ignored. On 8% battery this is the moment the app loses the person to Uber. The Landed card (`today/Landed.tsx`) has a good arrival checklist (phone, passport, bags, money, driver, hotel) but only renders on a trip's landed phase, not from Ask, and only if a pickup was pre-booked.
- No low-battery mode exists (grep "battery|low power" finds only a clock comment). Flighty/Apple's pattern: a Live Activity with the driver's name and plate, visible without unlocking; Careem's: "driver arrived" with a photo and plate.
- Verdict: 2/10 for the live moment.

### Scenario 12 — Talk to a human right now at 2 am (`s12-*.png`)
- Good: Help lists WhatsApp (+966 56 668 2662) and Call with "Day and night, in Arabic or English"; the support thread header says "Replies within 10 minutes, any hour"; offline, the support screen offers Call / SMS.

### Scenario 2 continued — the pay sheet, reached with account 0102 ("Istanbul for Eid for the family", `s2j-*.png`)
- Good, and genuinely better than Almosafer/Booking: "Price held · 19:41 left"; "SAR 4,320 · Everything included. No fees later."; "If you cancel, the flights come back minus SAR 400 per person."; Tabby 4 × 1,080 / Tamara 3 × 1,440 shown as plain lines; "Book now and add [passports] after. This is the Airbnb/Booking clarity pattern done right. `s2j-02-pay-s0..s3.png`.
- CM-2.8 "for the family" on an account whose household is "You, Test" (two adults) gave "2 people" — correct for that household, but it shows the rule: the app books *who is in the household*, and a household with no children cannot be told about children at booking time. The mother's path is therefore: leave the flow → Wallet/Household → add each child → scan each passport (to get a date of birth) → come back and search again. Five screens and three passports before a price is right.
- CM-2.9 The pay sheet never states the fare rules (bags, change fee), seats together, meal, or loyalty number applied (OWNER-AUDIT 5–6). "Everything included" is reassuring but a family wants "2 × 23 kg each, seats together requested, child meals × 2".
- No hotel on the sheet because the Galata bundle was not added on this account; when it is, the line is the hard-coded "near Galata Tower" (OWNER-AUDIT 11).

### Scenario 4 — The price changes between search and pay (code only; not triggerable on staging)
- Server: `POST /orders` compares `expectedTotal` with a fresh preview and answers `outcome: price_changed` with `previousTotal`, `preview`, `changedBy` (`platform/src/lib/app/booking/orders.ts:98`); also `hold_ended`.
- App: the pay screen opens a sheet "The price went up {price} while we checked. Airlines change fares minute to minute. Nothing was charged." with **Book at {price}** / **See other options** (`pay/index.tsx:190,378`; copy `pay.price.*`). The slider is not auto-retried. This is the right pattern (Kayak/Skyscanner's #6 pain is the price silently changing after redirect; here it is explicit and nothing is charged).
- Gaps: (a) the sheet has no "down" case: `changedBy` can be negative but the title says "went up"; (b) no "hold the old price for 10 min while I ask my husband" (Hopper's freeze, done honestly through the desk); (c) the 20-minute hold is not explained before it ends (what happens at 0:00?), and the "Hold ended" state copy is a two-word label; (d) a 3-D Secure OTP page sits inside this flow (`otp-3ds`) — the number-one Saudi payment failure mode (bank OTP never arrives abroad) has no "OTP didn't come" branch in the copy (grep finds none).
- Verdict: 7/10 on design, unverified on behaviour.

### Scenario 7 — A visa is refused (`s7*-*.png`)
- Ask: "I need a UK visa for me and my wife in December" → "For who? Noura · Add someone" (wife dropped, same parser) with a live passport check ("Expires 1 Jan 2027, under 6 months… Needs a look") → "When do you travel? Within a month · In 1 to 3 months · Not sure yet" (**"December" ignored**) → "Anything else we should know? Mada will find the earliest appointment, prepare the forms and reply here. · Send to Mada". The desk's quote pattern is good: "The earliest appointment is Tue 27 Oct, 10:20 at VFS Riyadh. I'll book it and prepare every form. SAR 450, plus the embassy fee paid on the day. · Pay SAR 450" (`st-02-requests.png`).
- CM-7.1 **A refusal has no state.** `BookingRequestStatus` is queued/sent/reviewing/quoted/paid/done/cancelled; "done" covers both "visa issued" and "visa refused". The traveller sees "Done" in the Requests list with no outcome, no refusal reason, no appeal deadline (15–30 days, Part 1a #19), no "what about my flights and hotel?" link, no refund trigger for the linked trip. The desk's free text is the only channel.
- CM-7.2 A finished visa thread rendered blank except "Back" in the live run (request fetch aborted, `s7d-01-visa-thread.png`); on retry via the list it loads. A thread that loads empty with no skeleton or retry is a dead end.
- CM-7.3 Mada does not know the UK ETA vs visa distinction for Saudi passports, nor that an ETA is lost on passport renewal (RESEARCH.md §4), yet the same account has a passport-renewal request open. The two requests do not talk to each other.
- Verdict: 4/10. Borrow: Nusuk's explicit statuses, and the airline "schedule change: accept / reject" card pattern — a refusal card with reason, deadline, "Appeal with Mada · SAR x", "Reapply", "Cancel the trip (what you get back: SAR y)".

### Request threads in general (`s7d-02-passport-thread.png`)
- The passport-renewal request from scenario 2 got a reply within minutes signed "Faisal, your Mada agent · I'll take care of it. SAR 150, and I'll send you the document here once it's issued." On staging that is a scripted desk; the risk in production is the one COPY.md warns about: a line that says "I" from a named person must come from that person. If the desk auto-quotes, sign it "Mada" not "Faisal".
- Nothing a traveller has sent can be withdrawn (OWNER-AUDIT 31). Still true.

### The 15 highest-leverage changes, in order

1. **Never drop a traveller silently.** When the parser cannot map counts or ages to the household, stop and ask: "2 adults and 3 children (2, 6, 13)? Who are they?" with a stepper (adults / children with ages / infants on lap or seat) before searching. Effort **M**. Evidence: CM-2.1, CM-10.2, CM-7 ("my wife"), parseWho `booking-logic.ts:130`; Google Flights' traveller stepper; family = 20% of summer outbound (Visa).
2. **Children need an age, not a passport.** "Add someone" gets a date of birth (or an age chip: baby / child / teen / adult); child and infant fares and "seats together" follow from it; the passport scan later overwrites it. Effort **S–M**. Evidence: CM-2.3, Part 1a #11–12.
3. **Make the Ask bar understand corrections on any flow.** "we are 2 adults and 3 children", "December", "right now", "I have a permit" must change the search, the request or the date, with a reply that says what changed. Replace the hotel-flavoured error with a generic "I didn't catch that. Did you mean to change who, when or where?" with chips. Effort **M**. Evidence: CM-2.2, CM-3.1, CM-6.1, CM-7 dates.
4. **A "now" path for cars and help.** From Ask or Today: "Car now" → driver name, plate, meeting point, price, "Faisal is watching"; "urgent" + a trip within 24 h → the trip's disruption card and a call-back button, not an ambulance number. Effort **M**. Evidence: CM-6.1, CM-12.1, Careem complaints (Part 1a #21).
5. **Name the hotel in the bundle and on every stay line.** "2 rooms at {hotel} · 6 nights · SAR 3,674 · free until 2 Mar" with a tap to the hotel page, on the results, pay sheet, trip and Today. Effort **S–M**. Evidence: CM-2.6, OWNER-AUDIT 11, Umrah "near the Haram" complaint (#18).
6. **Every request gets an outcome, and every outcome gets a next step.** Add `outcome: issued | refused | partial | cancelled` with reason, deadline and linked actions (appeal, reapply, refund the trip, change dates). Show "Mada is on it" only with "what happens next · by when · cost". Effort **M**. Evidence: CM-2.5, CM-7.1, OWNER-AUDIT 9 (cancelled trips vanish).
7. **Umrah flow built around the permit.** Ask for the permit date/slot (or offer to get one once Nusuk opens), then price Makkah nights by distance band with the hotel named, Madinah, Haramain train, and the last-ten-nights warning; drop the wheelchair checklist to an optional "needs" chip. Effort **M**. Evidence: CM-3.1–3.3, Part 1a #17–18, Nusuk rules (1c).
8. **Let guests see prices and real content.** Search flights and stays without an account; Discover defaults to the phone's city; plan/hotel/support links render for guests; one credentials line on Welcome. Effort **M**. Evidence: CM-1.1–1.4, HIG "explore before account".
9. **Language switch without losing the flow; reply in the traveller's language.** Persist the Ask state across the restart (or switch without restart on web); detect Arabic input and answer in Arabic; render notifications in the reader's language. Effort **M**. Evidence: CM-10.1–10.2, OWNER-AUDIT 18.
10. **Disruption screen that tells the truth.** When there is no disruption, say "Nothing is wrong with SV263. If that changes, this is where the options appear." and show the GACA rights card; keep "No signal" for real offline. Effort **S**. Evidence: CM-5.1.
11. **Family ranking and family lines on the three options.** When children are on the search: no 02:10 "Earliest" as a recommendation, "seats together" and "bassinet requested" lines, child meals, and the per-person breakdown (adult / child / infant) on the card and the pay sheet. Effort **M**. Evidence: CM-2.4, CM-2.9, Part 1a #11.
12. **Apply the preferences and loyalty numbers you collect, or stop promising.** Show "Applied: halal × 5, seats together, Alfursan 1234" on the pay sheet. Effort **M**. Evidence: OWNER-AUDIT 5–6; Saudia miles complaints (Part 1a #16).
13. **One money truth.** Deduplicate saved cards, make Wallet › Cards, Profile › Paying and the refund destination read the same source; add a trip Money view (total, per person, who paid, export). Effort **S** (cards) + **M** (money view). Evidence: four "Visa ending 11", CM-9.1, OWNER-AUDIT 7.
14. **Web app shell and a low-battery mode.** Service worker so a reload at the airport opens the cached trip; a one-screen "8% mode" (boarding pass, driver, hotel address, desk number) reachable from Today. Effort **M**. Evidence: CM-11.1, scenario 6.
15. **Live Activity + real boarding passes + Apple Wallet.** The Flighty standard: lock-screen gate/seat/leave-in, passes from airline check-in, pass updates on gate change. Effort **L**. Evidence: OWNER-AUDIT 13–14, Flighty press, Part 1a #15, #20.

Not in the 15 but still worth doing: "Tap again for bags and change rules" → a visible "Rules" link (S); the "Hold ended" and price-down copy (S); 3-D Secure "code didn't come" branch (S); sign scripted desk replies as "Mada", not a named agent (S); cancel/withdraw a sent request (M).
